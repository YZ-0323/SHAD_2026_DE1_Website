Prototype & Science

Discover our geometric fabric revolution: utilizing auxetic principles and plant-based structural elements to redefine stretch, strength, and durability in apparel.

01 — Structural Mechanics

Auxetic Geometry & Lateral Expansion

Under longitudinal tension, the geometric lattice expands laterally by +26% width, providing adaptive volume without fiber necking.

02 — Smart Internal Design

Rotating Panels & Tensile Strength

Corner-connected rigid squares rotate freely under strain, backed by high-tenacity lyocell fibers reaching 38–40 cN/tex strength.

03 — Material Integrity

Sustainable Closed-Loop Profile

Engineered from FSC-certified lyocell and natural rubber, offering 100% plant-based compostability within months of end-of-life.

04 — Stress Distribution

Targeted Tensile Reinforcement

Reinforced geometric grids absorb up to 98% of dynamic movement stress, preserving structural alignment across high-impact body zones.

05 — Elastic Memory

Reversible Elastic Memory

Natural latex hinges retain shape memory through 5,000+ extension cycles, replacing petroleum-derived elastane blends seamlessly.

06 — Ventilation

Micro-Mesh & Breathability

Dynamic geometric openings widen during active motion, boosting airflow permeability by 45% for superior moisture control.

07 — Performance Integration

Engineered for Demanding Performance

Integrating geometric textiles into real performance wear: articulated back stretch grids, shoulder flex zones, and multi-joint sleeve freedom for active movement.
